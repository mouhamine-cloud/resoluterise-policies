Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

ResoluteRise Publisher is a personal tool used by the account owner to publish short videos to the owner’s TikTok account. This policy explains what the tool handles and why.

Information the service handles

  • TikTok authorization data: When you authorize the service, TikTok provides account identifiers and access credentials such as access and refresh tokens. The service uses them to make the TikTok API requests you authorize.
  • Video and caption content: The service handles the video files and captions selected for publishing. It sends them to TikTok only when the account owner requests a post.
  • Operational records: The service keeps limited local records needed to avoid duplicate posts and check publishing status, such as a TikTok publish identifier and publishing time.

How information is used

The information above is used only to authorize the TikTok account, upload requested videos, check their processing status, and prevent accidental duplicate publishing. The service does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Sharing

Information is sent to TikTok when required to perform an authorized TikTok API operation. The service does not otherwise sell or share the information with data brokers. Hosting providers and infrastructure may process requests needed to deliver this website.

Storage and deletion

Authorization data and publishing records are stored locally by the account owner’s publishing tool. The account owner may delete local records and revoke the TikTok authorization at any time. Content already published to TikTok is governed by TikTok’s systems and policies.

Security

Reasonable steps are used to protect locally stored authorization data, but no electronic system can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not share an app secret or access token with anyone who should not control the TikTok account.

Children

This personal publishing tool is not directed to children under 13, and it is not intended for use by children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the service changes. The effective date above will be updated with material changes.
